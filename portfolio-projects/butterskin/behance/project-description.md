**Title:** ButterSkin: Botanical Skincare Brand, Packaging & Shopping App (Concept)

**Cover text:** Real care for real skin.

**Description (paste into Behance):**

ButterSkin is a self-initiated concept for a premium botanical skincare label and its own shopping app. The identity pairs a line-drawn lotus with Playfair Display, in deep emerald, sage, ivory and champagne. The packaging family covers eight fictional essentials in emerald and frosted glass, porcelain white and soft-touch tubes, finished with champagne metal.

The app lets you discover products by routine step or skin focus, read key ingredients in plain words, build a morning and evening routine, keep a private skin journal, and move from Add to bag through a bag and a short demo checkout. There are no ratings, certification badges or results claims.

Concept disclaimer: ButterSkin is fictional. Products, formulations and prices are illustrative. Product images are photorealistic 3D renders made in Blender, not photographs. The checkout is a demo, so nothing is sold or charged. Nothing here is medical advice, and no user research or testing was carried out.

Concept & art direction: Purnananda Mishra · AI-assisted design and production

**Tools used:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Blender, Playwright
