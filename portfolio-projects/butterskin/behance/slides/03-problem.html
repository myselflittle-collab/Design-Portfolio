The problem · assumptions

Skincare apps often sell perfection

Patterns this concept designs against

  • Streaks and scores that turn care into performance
  • "Before and after" promises and miracle claims
  • Long, intimidating ingredient lists with no context
  • Diagnosis-like quizzes that feel clinical

Framed from general product observation, as design assumptions, not research findings.

Design principles

  1. Skipping is fine. Checklists, never scores
  2. Where it fits, not what it fixes
  3. Personal, not prescriptive. Preferences, not diagnosis
  4. Calm by default. Soft colour, plain words
  5. Honest content. Fictional and concept labels where they matter
