Overview

Your skin, understood. Not judged.

ButterSkin is a concept for a skincare companion app: keep a simple routine, find everyday essentials and note how your skin feels, without scores, streaks or promises.

The project covers a brand identity, a packaging system for eight fictional products, design tokens, a responsive prototype with 22 screens and states, automated accessibility and behaviour checks, and this case study.

Concept disclaimer

ButterSkin is fictional. Products, formulations, prices, people and journal entries are illustrative. Nothing is sold, stored or sent, and nothing here is medical advice. No user research or usability testing was carried out.

Concept & art direction: Purnananda Mishra · AI-assisted design and production
