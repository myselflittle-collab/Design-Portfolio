**Title:** MoneyMines: Rewards App Concept (Brand & UX/UI)

**Tagline (cover text):** A rewards app that shows its working.

**Description (paste into Behance):**

MoneyMines is a self-initiated concept for an everyday payments app in which some transactions earn rewards: partner cashback, bill bonuses, scan-and-pay points and referrals.

The design question was trust. Reward features often merge real money with "maybe" money, hide the rules until after you pay, and push urgency. MoneyMines does the opposite:
- pending rewards are dashed ("not yet yours")
- wallet funds, confirmed rewards, pending rewards and points are never added together
- every reward shows its calculation
- eligibility is explained before paying.

The project includes the Strike identity, a token-based design system, 51 connected screens and states, a clickable prototype, and documented reward rules behind every number.

Concept disclaimer: MoneyMines is fictional. No bank, UPI, payment gateway or financial API is connected, all data is demonstration data, and no user research or testing was carried out.
