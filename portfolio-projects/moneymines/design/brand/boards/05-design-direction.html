Design direction: a field survey, not a casino

01
Dashed means not yet yours
Pending rewards get an outline, never a solid fill, so they can't be mistaken for usable money.
02
Every reward explains itself
The calculation, the cap and the rule ID appear wherever an amount appears.
03
Balances never merge
Wallet, confirmed, pending and points sit side by side, with no grand total.
04
Calm over urgent
Expiry dates are facts, not timers. No confetti, no streaks, no countdowns.
05
Simulated, and says so
Every confirm button and receipt carries the demo notice.
