Three routes to the mark

Each concept starts from the same idea: rewards are found inside everyday transactions, not handed out. No coins, no gradients, no treasure clichés.

A · Seam
40 · 28 · 20 px

The M as a cross-section of ground, cut by one tilted seam of lime ore. Layered, calm, geological.

Strong story, but the flat top reads close to an envelope at small sizes.

B · StrikeRecommended
40 · 28 · 20 px

Two shafts angle down to a lime facet, the moment a reward is found. The M stays readable at every size.

Clearest at 20 px. The facet becomes a reusable reward marker in the UI.

C · Bench
40 · 28 · 20 px

A terraced open pit. The deepest bench holds the reward, suggesting progress step by step.

Distinctive, but reads as a U before an M. Better as a pattern than a mark.
